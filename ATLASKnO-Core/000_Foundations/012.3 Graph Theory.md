# 012.3 Graph Theory

## Scope Note

Formal study of graphs — sets of vertices connected by edges — representing pairwise relations. Includes planarity, connectivity, spectral theory, and random graphs.

## History Note

Originated from Euler (1736) Seven Bridges of Königsberg problem.
